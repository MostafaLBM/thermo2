FROM THE PICTURE TO THE LAW

Pressures add

Dalton’s law: the pressure of a gas mixture is the sum of the pressures each gas would exert alone in the same volume at the same temperature.

P = PA + PB,   Pi = xiP,   xi = Ni/N

For point particles each species ignores the other, so removing one gas leaves the other’s pressure unchanged. For finite-size particles, measured species wall-force contributions can differ from their ideal values; they are not the pressures of each gas alone. Finite sampling and the approximate collision solver also cause deviations. Raise σ or N, or shrink the box, to see it.

Model, limits & further reading

Hard disks of equal diameter σ in a 2D box with elastic walls; σ = 0 gives the non-interacting ideal gas. Pressure is the time-averaged momentum transfer to the walls per unit time and wall length. η = Nπσ²/(4A) is the area fraction. Units are reduced (kB = 1), with masses 1 and 4. Collisions are elastic and mass-weighted. Readings need a couple of seconds to settle after any change.

Reading: Çengel, Boles & Kanoglu, Thermodynamics: An Engineering Approach, 9th ed., Chapter 13.
