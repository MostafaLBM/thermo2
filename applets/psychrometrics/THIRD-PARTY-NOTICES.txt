Moist-air property correlations
-------------------------------
Saturation pressure over water and ice (Hyland and Wexler), humidity ratio,
enthalpy, specific volume, thermodynamic wet bulb and the standard-atmosphere
pressure relation are implemented from the equations published in
ASHRAE Handbook - Fundamentals (2017), chapter 1, Psychrometrics, and
ASHRAE Chart No. 1 conventions (0-50 degC, 0-30 g/kg). No ASHRAE code or
tabulated data is included; the equations were re-implemented independently.

Known difference from ASHRAE tables: the water-vapor enhancement factor is
taken as 1, which lowers the saturation humidity ratio by about 0.4 %
(for example 14.70 vs 14.76 g/kg at 20 degC, 101.325 kPa).

No third-party JavaScript libraries are used.
